# Fashion Brand Website Features

## 🛍️ Product Showcase
- High-quality images for each product (multiple angles)
- Zoom feature + optional 360° view
- Short videos showing the product in use
- Product categorization (Men / Women / Kids / Collections)
- Advanced filtering (price, color, size, type)

---

## 📦 Product Details
- Available sizes with clear size chart
- Fabric/material information (Cotton, Polyester, etc.)
- Simple and clear product description
- Stock availability indicator
- Related / similar products suggestions

---

## 🛒 Cart & Checkout (E-commerce Core)
- Add to cart functionality
- Fast checkout flow
- Payment options:
  - Cash on delivery
  - Card / Mobile payments (e.g. Vodafone Cash)
- Automatic shipping cost calculation based on location
- Discount / coupon codes system

---

## 👤 User Accounts
- Sign up / Login (Email / Phone / Google)
- Order tracking system
- Wishlist (save favorite items)
- Easy re-order functionality

---

## 🚚 Shipping & Delivery
- Real-time order tracking
- Shipping company selection
- Estimated delivery time display
- SMS / WhatsApp notifications

---

## 🎯 Marketing Features
- Promotional pop-ups
- Sales & flash deals sections
- Countdown timers for offers
- Smart product recommendations (AI-based)

---

## 💬 Customer Support
- Live chat system
- Quick WhatsApp contact button
- FAQ section
- Return & exchange policy page

## 📱 Mobile Experience
- Mobile-first responsive design
